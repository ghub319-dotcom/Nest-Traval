❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What destinations are available on TravelNest? ▼

TravelNest currently features 10 handpicked destinations from around the world, including Bali, Paris, Tokyo, New Zealand, Barcelona, Thailand, Iceland, Morocco, Costa Rica, and Egypt. Each destination comes with detailed information about attractions, costs, and travel tips.

How does the Budget Planner work? ▼

The Budget Planner is simple to use: 1) Select your destination, 2) Enter the number of days, 3) Input your daily budget. Our tool will instantly calculate your total trip cost and provide a budget status (budget-friendly, moderate, or luxury). You can save your calculated budgets for future reference.

Can I save my preferences and data? ▼

Yes! TravelNest uses localStorage to save all your data locally on your device. This includes your wishlist, saved budgets, visited destinations, planned trips, and newsletter subscriptions. Your data is secure and accessible whenever you visit the site.

How does the Random Trip Generator work? ▼

The Random Trip Generator uses your preferences (travel type and budget range) to suggest destinations that match your criteria. Select your preferred travel style (adventure, relaxation, cultural, or nature) and budget level (low, medium, or high), then let our algorithm surprise you with a personalized suggestion!

Is TravelNest available on mobile devices? ▼

Absolutely! TravelNest is fully responsive and works seamlessly on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers. We've optimized the design for all screen sizes, making it easy to plan your trips on the go.

What about the Travel Mood feature? ▼

The Travel Mood Curator allows you to immerse yourself in travel vibes with ambient sounds from different environments (beach, forest, city). You can also track your travel aspirations by marking destinations as visited or planned, maintaining a personal travel journal and bucket list.

Is there a newsletter subscription? ▼

Yes! You can subscribe to our newsletter from the footer of any page. We share travel tips, destination highlights, budget hacks, and exclusive travel inspiration. Your email is stored securely and we never spam.

Can I filter destinations by continent? ▼

Yes! On the Destination Explorer page, you can filter destinations by continent (Asia, Europe, Africa, Americas, Oceania) or search by destination name. This makes it easy to find destinations in your preferred region.

How is my data protected? ▼

All your personal data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. No information is sent to external servers. Your data remains completely private and under your control. You can clear your data anytime through your browser settings.
